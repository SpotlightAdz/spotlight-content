# Spotlight viral content playbook

The production toolkit and the full standard live in the repo `SpotlightAdz/spotlight-content` → `tools/STYLE.md`. Read it first when you have the repo; this skill is the short version for planning and review.

## What Instagram rewards (Mosseri, Jan 2025)
- Top signals: **watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach**. Sends matter most for reaching **non-followers** → design content people DM to someone specific.
- Original wins: no reposts, no TikTok/CapCut watermarks (excluded from Explore), no unlabelled AI people.
- Caption text is used for search → first line = the hook + the service + the city, in Darija.
- Unswiped carousels get shown again from the first unseen slide → slide 2 must stand alone as a cover.
- Reels up to 3 min can be recommended; test one longer story reel a week. Trial Reels test hooks on non-followers.

## Reel checklist (score each 0–2; ship at 16+/20)
1. Frame 0 = hook text + hero visual, readable with sound off.
2. Hook spoken in the first 2 s, in Darija, about the viewer's problem (not about us).
3. One idea. 3–5 beats. A pattern break every 2–3 s.
4. Payoff held to the end ("التالتة هي اللي…").
5. Proof: client name, handle or number from the facts list; invented UI labelled مثال توضيحي.
6. Captions word-synced, active word lit, inside the safe zone (y 220–1480).
7. Sound: voice ≥10 dB over music, hit on frame 0, tick on cuts, riser peaking on the CTA cut, -14 LUFS, original music only (or Instagram's licensed audio added at scheduling).
8. Ends on ONE action, ideally a send ("صيفطها لـ…"), and loops seamlessly into the hook.
9. Brand colours, 60-30-10, contrast checked (white on #25D366 fails; use #0E7A43).
10. It looks finished next to a top agency's feed.

## Carousel checklist
7–10 slides · slide 1 number + promise · slide 2 a second cover · ≤35 words per slide · one idea per slide · body ≥44 px · save cue · last slide one CTA · RTL Arabic, LTR numbers/handles in their own element · no «» around Arabic.

## Formats to rotate
Mistakes/myth explainer · client result story · behind the scenes · owner talking head (highest trust) · relatable POV · service launch · editorial carousels (lists, frameworks, pricing, case studies).

## Hook patterns that work in Darija
- Number + loss: "3 غلطات كيخسروك الكليان"
- Myth + "لا": "خاصك 10K متابع باش تبيع؟ لا."
- POV: "POV: بشحال؟ للمرة 47 اليوم"
- Result first: "101 ألف متابع… و هادي غير البداية"
- Identity call-out: "إلا عندك ريسطو / صالون / حانوت…"
